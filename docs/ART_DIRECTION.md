# SIXTY SIX — Editorial direction

## Reference study

Reviewed CHALK’s homepage, Femme menu, artists page and client gallery on 14 October 2026: https://www.chalk.ae/ , https://www.chalk.ae/femme , https://www.chalk.ae/artists , https://www.chalk.ae/influencers .

The useful principles are image dominance, changes in photographic scale, generous separation between sections, bold type, portrait-led artist presentation and a service menu with simple hierarchy. These create personality and rhythm without elaborate UI. SIXTY SIX will use original compositions and independently sourced photography; no CHALK assets, code, branding or business claims are reused.

## Visual system

Soft ivory #EDE9E2, warm stone #DCD5CA and charcoal #191A19 form the base. Muted bronze #AA9272 and taupe #A79A8D appear in small labels, rules and borders. Espresso #6C5745 supports the warm neutral system; the client’s logo retains its original red. Locally hosted Playfair Display provides high-contrast editorial headings; DM Sans carries navigation, captions and forms. Manrope is reserved for the wordmark.

## Photography and composition

Use locally downloaded, optimized Pexels photography with source, photographer and license recorded in the image manifest. Select natural skin texture, polished hair, muted warm tones, architectural lines and precise barbering, beard detailing, male skincare and grooming tools. Use full-height campaign portraits, an asymmetric six-category composition, paired interior views, cinematic breaks and an expansive nine-image gallery. Avoid presenting stock models as employees or stock treatments as client results. All reference imagery is identified as an editorial preview.

## Page rhythm

Home: photograph-led campaign → airy architectural introduction → staggered service images → full-width portrait → dark studio narrative → immersive gallery → quiet transformations notice → launch campaign → priority form → concise FAQ → location → oversized dark wordmark.

Services: cinematic photograph → sticky category index → alternating portrait/menu layouts, with occasional secondary detail images → personal consultation CTA. Prices are “on request” until a confirmed catalogue is supplied.

## Mobile and interaction

Use a dedicated hero crop, compact overlay navigation, a two-column service collage with deliberate wide features, horizontal gallery browsing and full-width catalogue photography. Preserve comfortable labels and 44px touch targets. Avoid massive headings, clipping and image/text overlap. Dialogs trap focus, close on Escape and restore focus. Gallery supports next/previous, arrow keys and touch swipe. Motion is limited to small reveal/hover effects and is removed with reduced-motion preferences.
